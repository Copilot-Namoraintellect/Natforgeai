## 2. A08 monitoring mapping

### Identity and interface rules

A04 role labels below are proposed logical duties, not provisioned accounts: MON is the limited collector; API/WRK/SCH own only their service facts; BKP emits redacted backup facts; STOP is independent incident/H administration. E-INGEST denotes a trusted telemetry persistence component requiring a separately assigned identity and narrow insert/update rights on E projections only. It is not MON with arbitrary business SQL. Actual principals, endpoint authentication and writer deployment ownership are UNASSIGNED under D05/D07/D09/D12.

MON may query approved service status/read-only counters and redacted exports. It cannot restart, reconfigure or debug processes, enumerate environments, inspect backup contents or use runtime credentials. A protected producer interface supplies safe facts; exact transport, authentication, cadence, timeout, freshness budget and schema adaptation require approval. Exception/missing permission/unimplemented interface returns null/UNKNOWN with bounded reason, not zero/healthy. Known measured failure is retained as failure; it is not disguised as absent telemetry.

## 3. A09 telemetry behavior

### Evidence validity and identity

Binding envelope proposal: schema/contract version, trusted source role and allowlisted source label, exact environment/service/instance/process-generation/release reference, server-resolved tenant/business scope where applicable, explicit occurredAt/observedAt, sample interval/units/counts, collector availability/freshness and safe bounded facts. This is a future adapter requirement, not an existing executable JSON config or a replacement E schema. Collector availability UNKNOWN and measured target DOWN must be separate. Untrusted input cannot set health, scope, release, suppression authority or execution outcomes. Observations from replaced process generations/releases cannot satisfy current instance health.

Absent collector/permission/timeout/null/invalid schema -> affected dimension UNKNOWN; retain source reason and last successful sample timestamp as historical, never continue cached HEALTHY indefinitely. Approved freshness budget expiry -> current evidence unavailable/UNKNOWN; a current observation proving stale worker heartbeat retains E's DEGRADED/UNAVAILABLE result. These are distinct cases. Platform aggregate preserves E's unknownDimensions and known worse health: UNKNOWN does not mask AT_RISK/UNAVAILABLE, and a healthy subset never certifies the whole platform. H consumes required evidence through trusted composition, which denies/reduces safely; this spec grants MON no latch/control capability.

E-IDENTITY already uses occurredAt among material event coordinates and observedAt for health-observation identity. Preserve those explicit source coordinates and normalization; E-PLATFORM's “no timestamps/randomness added” forbids new collection-time salt, not existing contract-defined timestamps. Exact retransmission retains the same event identity/fingerprint and converges once. A genuinely separate occurrence has its own explicit source occurrence/time/safe fact coordinates; never mutate timestamp just to bypass replay. Same identity with conflicting immutable facts fails closed and is observable.

### Alerts, delivery, suppression and release correlation

N distinct repeated failures with one failure target produce N preserved raw failure events and one open counted alert with occurrenceCount=N; audit/lifecycle/fold events are additional evidence, not subtracted to force a total row count of N. Replaying one identical occurrence does not inflate N. Preserve correlationId without inventing one. Identity scopes, userScope/store predicates and target key construction must prevent tenant/business folds; E-ALERT's environment+dedup-target shape alone is not evidence of end-to-end scope isolation. E/X must prove scoped target/store binding and remedy any collision under separately approved implementation. No per-tenant alert counts may disclose platform or sibling data. Bounded fold/re-arm policy remains D09 approval; resolution never deletes history or resets authority.

Delivery is an external effect limited to notifications, not business/runtime action. Proposed durable outbox uses existing alert identity plus approved route and delivery-attempt identity; replay must not double-count a failure occurrence or falsely claim delivery. Bound retries/backoff/timeouts and distinguish accepted by transport from acknowledged by receiver. Failed/unknown delivery appends a bounded platform operational event using diagnosticCode UNKNOWN as E-PLATFORM requires; exact closed eventType mapping, retention and retry sender owner need E approval. Do not invent `alert_delivery_failed` as a currently supported event type. If primary store/transport is unavailable, protected bounded independent spool/heartbeat reports the gap; exhausted storage/fallback produces visible UNKNOWN/undelivered state and human escalation via a separately approved route, not infinite recursion through the same failed sender. Outbox/spool are unimplemented proposals; no test notification is sent here.

Suppression requires server-verified admin/operator context, non-empty authority reference, reason, permitted scope/transition and append-only audit; CRITICAL is never suppressible. Unauthorized or audit-failed suppression changes nothing. Dedup is not suppression. G backup alerts cannot be silenced/acked/rerouted by application runtime, worker, agent or environment flag; external monitoring-operator routing authority is separate. Exact roles, expiry and recovery of suppressed state need D09/D12 review. A suppressed incident retains events, safety status and authority/audit facts; suppression cannot make health healthy or turn on execution. No automatic resolution on absent collector. Recovery requires fresh positive evidence and governed lifecycle transition, with historical uncertainty retained.

Release attribution originates in verified A07 manifest and trusted supervisor injection of RELEASE_VERSION/GIT_SHA, plus service generation/role. E null-coalescing does not authenticate arbitrary env values; future adapter must verify manifest association and reject blank/drift values. New/restarted API, worker and scheduler each need new current evidence; matching version without heartbeat is UNKNOWN. Mixed release/worker version drift visible; telemetry never performs rollout/restart or sets H level. H observation's actualExecution remains false; shadow/canary reservations are never successful dispatches. Healthy recovery never clears H safe-mode/independent-stop latch or grants R9-F approval.

## 4. A10 monitoring security

Every tenant/business read and write attribution resolves ownership from authenticated server principal; never trust client tenantId/userScope/business ownership. Platform-scope null is admin-only, not wildcard tenant access. Scoped pagination, incidents, alerts, timeline, SLO, health caches, metrics labels, correlation and dedup must not leak counts/titles or join unrelated scopes. Mission/recovery aggregates from E default bridge are platform evidence: keep admin-only unless a verified tenant-predicated projection is supplied. Admin role membership is not a grant for MON to impersonate arbitrary tenants. Source authentication, replay bounds and exact ownership validation precede ingest; transport choice remains OPEN.

Apply F closed classification/reader sets and A05 redaction across safeMetadata, errors, callback/provider references, labels, route classes, log/spool/notification/audit exports. E depth <=6, size/string bounds, no Error/class objects; redaction/payload rejection occurs before persistence or delivery. No credentials, raw URLs/query strings, request/response bodies, social data, PII, chain-of-thought, connection strings or environment/process dumps. Use synthetic canaries only; report finding path/rule, never value or value-derived hashes. Restrict log access and keep crash dumps out of MON. No agent receives broker, secret store or notification credential handles. NOTIFY credentials need F-approved class/identity binding; SMTP/provider credentials are not automatically permitted to MON by F's monitoring infrastructure reader allowance. Do not broaden F or give MON runtime REDIS_URL/DATABASE_URL to simplify collection.

MON read-only observation differs from E trusted telemetry writes and human incident transitions. Separate authenticated roles/tokens, least-privilege telemetry persistence operations and append-only event/audit storage. No broad DB UPDATE/DELETE or upstream authority mutations. Audit material incident/suppression/routing/config/access-denial changes with actor class, scope, source/authority reference, UTC, previous/new state, release and correlation as safe labels. Exact E taxonomy/facts remain authoritative; new collector/config audit types require review rather than silently extending enums. Audit failure denies state-changing lifecycle/control changes; diagnostics about audit failure contain no sensitive payload. Protected independent audit/export integrity and retention are not supplied by ordinary writable spool files.

F and H boundaries remain default-deny: no monitoring endpoint/tool may retry/recover/execute, switch provider, change credentials, migrate, restart services, suppress backup, alter policy/autonomy, clear stop or edit execution/audit history. H controller observer is read-only exact-scope; absent configuration DISABLED/UNCONFIGURED version 0 and unavailable independent reader fails visibly. Trusted H runtime safety may reduce authority automatically on missing required monitoring/backup/security, under H semantics; telemetry never gets H admin handles. Independent STOP authority remains outside runtime/MON; fresh health cannot clear it. A07 rotation/issuer-custodian/approver gates remain OPEN and cannot be bypassed by a monitoring PASS.
