# Product Vision — NatForge AI

## Original Product Vision

> **Build the world's first fully autonomous marketing operating system — one that thinks, creates, executes, and optimises like a world-class marketing team, available to every business for the price of a software subscription.**

NatForge was conceived to solve a fundamental market inefficiency: the vast majority of businesses cannot afford expert marketing talent, yet they desperately need consistent, high-quality marketing to survive and grow. AI has reached the inflection point where a single platform can replicate the output of a strategy director, creative director, media buyer, community manager, and sales development representative — all working in concert.

## The Autonomous Marketing Operating System

NatForge is not a collection of AI tools. It is an **operating system** with:

- **Unified memory**: Every agent shares context about the business, campaign, and customer
- **Orchestrated execution**: Agents trigger each other in sequence based on workflow state
- **Continuous learning**: Performance data from publishing and engagement feeds back to strategy and creative
- **Safety guardrails**: Content safety checks, approval gates, and cost controls prevent autonomous errors
- **Transparent operation**: Users can inspect every AI decision, edit any output, and pause automation at any time

## Human-in-the-Loop Principle

Autonomy does not mean absence of human judgment. NatForge operates on a **human-in-the-loop** principle:

- **Humans set direction**: Business goals, brand tone, budget, approval mode
- **AI executes**: Agents produce outputs, schedule actions, and monitor results
- **Humans review at gates**: Critical decisions (launch, high-risk content, budget changes) require human approval
- **Humans override**: Any AI output can be edited, rejected, or regenerated

This principle ensures that autonomy delivers speed and scale without sacrificing brand safety or strategic alignment.

## Assisted vs Autonomous Mode

NatForge supports two operating modes that users can switch between per campaign:

### Assisted Mode (Default)
- Every major agent output requires human approval before proceeding
- Users see drafts, edit them, and approve/reject
- Best for: New users, brand-sensitive campaigns, high-budget campaigns
- **Safety**: Maximum — nothing publishes without explicit approval

### Autonomous Mode
- AI agents execute end-to-end with minimal human intervention
- Safety checks and cost controls still enforce boundaries
- Best for: Mature campaigns, trusted brand voice, high-volume operations
- **Safety**: High — content safety agent blocks risky material; cost controls enforce budgets

### Mode Switching
Users can change modes at any time:
- Assisted → Autonomous: When the user trusts the AI's output quality
- Autonomous → Assisted: When unusual circumstances require closer review (e.g., product launch, PR crisis)
